1 Executive Summary & Product Vision

General home-workout apps are a crowded, ad-funded market. Fit10 Mom narrows the original Fit10 product to one underserved group: women recovering after pregnancy. These users have time in small pieces (nap windows), real physical considerations (C-section healing, abdominal separation, pelvic floor changes), and chronic tiredness. Generic apps ignore all three.

Core Value Proposition
"Safe 10-minute workouts to get your strength and energy back after baby. Designed with physiotherapists. In your language."

The existing Fit10 engine (adaptive difficulty, exercise swaps, interval player, offline storage) is reused and redirected: swaps become postpartum-safe alternatives, the adaptation engine responds to sleep and energy, and random workouts become a guided 12-week recovery journey. That journey is also the main reason to pay.

3 Safety Framework (Non-Negotiable)

Launch blocker
All exercises, phase rules and screening questions must be reviewed and signed off by a qualified women's health physiotherapist before release. The app is a fitness product, not a medical device, and must never diagnose or replace clinical advice.
  • Medical clearance gate: The 12-week journey starts only after the user confirms her doctor has cleared her for exercise. Guidance text: typically around 6 weeks after a vaginal birth and 8 weeks or more after a C-section, but the doctor's advice always wins.
  • Not for pregnancy: Users who are currently pregnant are told the app is not designed for them.
  • Red-flag routing: Pain, bleeding, pelvic heaviness, or leaking that worsens with exercise stops the session and shows "please consult your doctor or a women's health physiotherapist". No workout is offered that day.
  • Contraindication tags: Every exercise carries tags (e.g. DIASTASIS_LOAD, PELVIC_FLOOR_IMPACT, EARLY_CSECTION, PRONE_PRESSURE). The engine filters exercises against the user's profile.
  • Excluded entirely: Crunches and bicycle crunches (they load the abdominal wall in a way that can worsen separation).
  • Gated to Phase 4 after self-check: Full planks, shoulder taps, jumping jacks, high knees, mountain climbers. A low-impact alternative is always one tap away.
  • Body-image care: No "bounce back" language, no before/after weight framing, weight tracking off by default.

4 Functional Requirements

4.1 Onboarding & Safety Screening (OnboardingViewModel)

  1. Language: Hindi or English (changeable later).
  2. Pregnancy status: Currently pregnant → polite exit message.
  3. Baby's age: Weeks or months since birth.
  4. Delivery type: Vaginal, C-section, or prefer not to say (treated as C-section for safety).
  5. Doctor clearance: Yes / Not yet / Not sure. "Not yet" unlocks only the gentle recovery basics.
  6. Body check: Guided abdominal separation self-check (video) and pelvic floor symptom questions (leaking, heaviness).
  7. Pre-pregnancy activity level: Inactive, somewhat active, very active.
  8. Goal: More energy, core strength, back pain relief, overall fitness.
  9. Schedule: Days per week and preferred reminder time (default: 5 days).

Output: a personalized plan summary ("You'll start in Phase 1: Reconnect, 3 weeks, 10 minutes a day"), immediately followed by the paywall (Section 9.2).

4.2 Daily Energy Check-In

Before each session: "How did you sleep?" (Badly / Okay / Well) and "Any pain or discomfort today?" (Yes → red-flag questions). A bad night switches the day's session to a gentler variant at the same phase. Logged locally to show patterns over time.

4.4 Workout Player (WorkoutSessionEngine changes)

  • Baby-proof pause: Session state is saved on pause and survives the app being closed. Resume within 2 hours continues where she left off; after that, offer "finish the last few exercises" or restart.
  • Hindi and English voice coaching (pre-recorded cues) in addition to tones and haptics.
  • Quiet mode: Haptics plus on-screen cues only, for when the baby is sleeping nearby.
  • Breathing cues on screen for every core and pelvic floor exercise (exhale on effort).

4.5 Session Library

  • 12-Week Recovery Journey (main program, Section 5).
  • Quick Energy (5 min): Standing, low-impact sessions for tired days.
  • Back & Neck Relief: For feeding and carrying posture.
  • With Baby: Babywearing squats, baby-on-chest bridges, playtime floor sessions.
  • Mom Strength (after week 12): Ongoing rotating sessions so the subscription keeps delivering value.

4.6 Progress & Motivation

  • Streaks that forgive missed days (a baby is unpredictable): 5 sessions per week counts as a full week.
  • Strength milestones ("first incline push-up", "Phase 2 complete"), energy trend from check-ins, optional private progress photos stored only on the device.
  • Shareable milestone cards for WhatsApp and Instagram (no health details on the card). This doubles as organic marketing.

5 Content: The 12-Week Recovery Journey

Four 3-week phases, 5 sessions of 10 minutes per week (60 sessions in total). The schedule is a guideline; phase gating (4.3) decides progression.

PhaseWeeksFocusExample Exercises
1 · Reconnect1–3Breathing, pelvic floor, posture, gentle mobility360° breathing, pelvic floor activation (quick and long holds), core connection breath, heel slides, pelvic tilts, clamshells, cat-cow, child's pose, neck & shoulder release
2 · Rebuild Core4–6Deep core and glutes, light upper bodyGlute bridge, kneeling bird dog, modified dead bug (toe taps), side-lying leg lifts, wall push-up, sit-to-stand from chair, side plank from knees
3 · Strength7–9Full-body strength for daily mom life (lifting, carrying)Bodyweight squat, reverse lunge, supported split squat, incline push-up, glute bridge march, single-leg bridge, wall sit, suitcase carry (water bottle or baby bag), babywearing squat
4 · Energy10–12Low-impact cardio, then impact only if readyMarching, step-touch, step jacks, incline plank, slow incline mountain climbers; jumping jacks and high knees only after pelvic floor readiness check

Catalog size: about 40 exercises. Roughly 14 safe exercises from the v1.0 catalog are reused (squats, reverse lunges, glute bridges, bridge march, wall sit, bird dog, cat-cow, child's pose, arm circles, and others); about 26 are new.

Video content
Stock preview clips in scratch_videos/ (e.g. iStock "adpp" files) are not licensed for release. Shoot original videos with a female trainer in modest workout clothing, ideally a mother herself. This is also stronger branding.

  • Appearance setting: System (default), Light, Dark. Dark mode is tuned for night feeds: low glare, soft pink accents.
  • No hardcoded colors in screens. Every color comes from the theme so both modes always work. Extra accents (streak, rest, timer) get light and dark values through a theme-provided Fit10ExtendedColors.
  • Contrast: text and buttons meet WCAG AA (4.5:1) in both modes.
  • Palette validation: show Plum, Rose and Lavender to beta users; later A/B test store screenshots with Play store listing experiments.

Layout & components

  • Edge-to-edge screens, large rounded cards (20dp), generous spacing, sentence-case copy.
  • Primary actions in the bottom third of the screen for one-handed use; touch targets of at least 48dp.
  • Shared-element transition: today's session card expands into the workout player.
  • Skeleton shimmer instead of spinners; animated bottom navigation icons.
  • Hindi typography with Noto Sans Devanagari; layouts tested at 200% font scale and with TalkBack.

9 Revenue Model & Monetization

Principle: safety basics are free; the guided transformation is paid. No ads. Ads would undermine trust with this audience and the privacy promise.

9.1 Pricing (Google Play Billing)

PlanPrice (INR)TermsWhy it exists
Free₹0No card required.Onboarding, safety screening, Phase 1 Week 1, daily breathing & pelvic floor routine, one Quick Energy session, reminders. Builds trust and the habit.
Annual (Recommended)₹699 / year7-day free trial, auto-renews. About ₹58 per month.Main revenue driver. Covers the full 12-week journey plus Mom Strength afterwards.
Monthly₹149 / monthCancel anytime.For users who don't want to commit. Makes the annual plan look like good value.
Lifetime₹1,499 one-timeIn-app product, no renewal.Many Indian users avoid subscriptions. Also easy for a husband or family member to buy as a gift on her phone.

Pro includes: the full 12-week journey (Phases 1–4), Mom Strength library, Back & Neck Relief, With Baby sessions, energy-adaptive sessions, unlimited safe swaps, milestones and progress history. Language and safety features are never behind the paywall.

Later experiment: a prepaid "12-Week Journey Pass" (₹399, no auto-renew) using Play's prepaid plans, A/B tested against the monthly plan.

9.2 Paywall Placement

  • After onboarding: Show her personalized plan, then the annual trial offer, with a clear "Continue free" option.
  • End of Phase 1 Week 1: Celebrate the first week, then "unlock the rest of your journey". This is the highest-intent moment.
  • Contextual: Tapping a Pro session opens the existing ContextualUpsellSheet.
  • Copy is warm and honest. No countdown timers, fake discounts, or shame-based messaging.

13 Roadmap

  • Pre-launch (6–8 weeks): Sign a physio partner, rewrite content, film original videos, closed beta with 100–200 moms. Test pricing and measure retention before a public launch.
  • v1.0 Launch: Hindi + English, 12-week journey, Annual / Monthly / Lifetime plans.
  • v1.1: Telugu, Tamil, Marathi, Bengali; prepaid 12-Week Pass experiment.
  • v1.2: Physio consultation booking (first backend component).
  • v1.3: Nutrition plans; clinic partner program.
  • v1.4: Moderated mom community; Health Connect sync (steps, sleep).
