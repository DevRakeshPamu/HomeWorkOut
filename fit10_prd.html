1 Executive Summary & Product Vision

Fit10 addresses the primary bottleneck in home fitness adherence: time friction and chronic burnout. While conventional fitness applications prescribe rigid 45–60 minute sessions requiring equipment or gyms, Fit10 delivers high-density, scientifically sequenced 10-minute micro-workouts performed entirely with bodyweight.

Core Value Proposition
"Ten minutes of scientifically programmed daily movement that adapts to your joint comfort, prevents overtraining through muscle recovery awareness, and builds an unbreakable exercise habit."

By integrating an automated deload cycle, muscle overlap protection (ensuring back-to-back workouts do not fatigue identical muscle groups), and real-time exercise substitution, Fit10 ensures every user makes steady progress without risk of overuse injury or demotivation.

4 Functional Requirements & Feature Specifications

4.1 Onboarding & Assessment Flow

A guided 6-step questionnaire implemented in OnboardingViewModel that captures the user's fitness profile and synthesizes their initial training program:

  • Step 1: Fitness Level: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced baseline.
  • Step 2: Primary Fitness Goal: Lose Weight, Build Strength, Improve Fitness, Flexibility, or Stay Active.
  • Step 3: Target Daily Duration: 5, 10 (default), or 15 minutes.
  • Step 4: Available Equipment: None / Bodyweight (default), Dumbbells, Resistance Bands, Yoga Mat.
  • Step 5: Exercise Exclusions / Physical Limitations: Avoid jumping, avoid knee stress, avoid wrist loading, or none.
  • Step 6: Weekly Schedule: Select active days per week (default 5 days) and recovery style (Active Recovery vs Complete Rest).

4.2 Intelligent Personalization & Adaptation Engine

The core algorithm (PersonalizationEngine) scores candidate workouts and recalibrates user volume using multi-factor heuristics:

Heuristic Rule Specification Impact on User
Deload Gate Every 20 completed sessions (total % 20 == 0), the engine forces 3–5 days of MOBILITY routines. Prevents chronic systemic fatigue and tendon strain; promotes active recovery.
Overlap-Aware Recovery Treats FULL_BODY as overlapping Lower, Upper, and Core. Back-to-back identical muscle stress applies a -15 penalty; alternating groups grants a +12 bonus. Prevents consecutive-day quadriceps or pushing overload.
Weekly Volume Guard Penalizes any muscle focus area appearing $\ge 2\times$ (or Full Body $\ge 3\times$) in the rolling 7 completed sessions with a -20 penalty. Enforces natural muscle rotation throughout the week.
Staleness Guard Penalizes the exact workout completed in the prior session with -8 penalty to break score ties. Guarantees routine variety even when user preferences are narrow.
Auto-Progression $\ge 3$ of last 5 sessions rated TOO_EASY with $\ge 95\%$ completion advances level to Intermediate/Advanced. Also triggers if 3 consecutive sessions hit 100% completion. Applies progressive overload without manual user configuration.
Auto-Regression $\ge 3$ ratings of TOO_HARD or completion rate $< 60\%$ drops difficulty by one tier and activates low-impact exercise substitutions. Protects struggling users from dropout and injury.

4.3 Real-Time Interval Workout Player

Powered by WorkoutSessionEngine operating on a dedicated coroutine ticker:

  • State Lifecycle: Idle → Countdown (5s prep) → ActiveExercise (30–60s) → RestInterval (15–20s) → Summary.
  • Interactive Controls: Play, Pause, Resume, Skip Next, Go to Previous Exercise.
  • Audio Cues: Synthesized beeps via Android ToneGenerator: countdown tick (TONE_PROP_BEEP), exercise start (TONE_PROP_ACK), rest interval (TONE_PROP_PROMPT), halfway mark, and completion fanfare.
  • Haptic Engine: Waveform vibration patterns coordinated by WorkoutFeedbackCoordinator for silent pocket/floor workout execution.
  • Visual Motion Guides: Animated demonstration component and live countdown circular progress rings.

4.4 Exercise Replacement & Regression Engine

Enables users to swap out problematic exercises prior to or during a workout. Substitutions preserve physiological intent while adjusting joint loading:

  • Basic High-Impact Swaps (Free Core Tier): Jumping Jacks (Moderate Impact) → Arm Circles Warm-up (Zero Impact), Mountain Climbers (Moderate Impact) → Forearm Plank Hold (Low Impact Core), High Knees (High Impact) → Glute Bridges (Low Impact Posterior Chain).
  • Full Joint-Friendly Regression Engine (Fit10 Pro): Unlocks infinite custom substitutions across all 21 exercises, knee/wrist/joint pain avoidance filters, and multi-tier progressions (e.g. Standard Push-up → Wide-Grip, Knee Push-up, Wall Push-up, or Decline Push-up).

5 Technical Architecture & Data Design

Architecture Philosophy: Clean MVVM + Unidirectional Data Flow (UDF)
Every feature is decomposed into UI (Compose), ViewModel (StateFlow), Domain (Use Cases / Engines), and Data (Room / DataStore). UI components observe immutable UI states and dispatch declarative events.

5.1 Database Entity Relationship (Room)

  • ExerciseEntity: Stores 21 canonical exercises (ID, name, instructions, tips, target muscles, equipment, impact level).
  • WorkoutEntity & WorkoutExerciseEntity: Represents workout metadata and joined exercise sequence with order index, duration, reps, and rest timers.
  • ProgramEntity & ProgramDayEntity: Multi-week tracks mapped to daily workout IDs and rest flags.
  • WorkoutResultEntity: Historical session logs (session ID, workout ID, duration, exercises completed, calories, RPE feedback rating, completed timestamp).

6 Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)

Dimension Target Standard Verification Method
Cold Start Latency $\le 800\text{ ms}$ to interactive Home screen. Baseline Profiles + R8 optimization.

Offline Resiliency 100% of workouts, timers, audio tones, and logs execute without internet. Room database pre-seeded on first run.
Thermal & Battery Load $< 2\%$ battery consumption per 10-minute session. Coroutine timer delays over wake-locks; low CPU audio synthesis.
Data Privacy Zero health/biometric data transmitted to remote servers by default. On-device Room storage; exportable JSON database.
